MUD Foundation Showcase -- Islamic Art

This was a project created as part of my coursework for Designing Virtual Worlds, in collaboration with the MUD Foundation and the Islamic Arts class at UM, which was among a number of others created by my fellow VW students.

The goal was to create an interactive virtual environment showcasing Islamic art within MUD Foundation's Javascript-based development platform for the Metaverse.

Collaborating with a fellow VW student (who helped procure the models) and an art history student (who provided the context and narration for the exhibit), I developed the virtual space and integrated multimedia elements to showcase various Islamic sculptures and their significance.
