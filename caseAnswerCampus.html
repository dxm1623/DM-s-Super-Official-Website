Case Study: "Answer Campus"

A snippet of one of the deliverables I created for NERDLab, featuring writing I did for one of the main characters.

Type: Writing & Narrative Design, Project Coordination | Duration: Ongoing

Product: Narrative Deliverables for Visual Novel

Answer Campus is an in-development visual novel that has been worked on by members of the newly formed NERDLab, an in-house game development studio operating within the University of Miami where I study. I was introduced to Answer Campus through my coursework and later would involve myself directly with the development of the game through NERDLab as part of my practicum works.

Answer Campus is a social impact game aimed at simulating a university student's social and academic experiences. Eager to delve deeper into such topics as well as to build my development and design skills, I joined NERDLab, entering the writing team for Answer Campus. Since then, I have contributed a number of deliverables in the form of written scripts for potential set pieces as well as playable prototypes within Unity. On top of this, I have helped contribute to the plotting and development of Answer Campus through frequent in-person and virtual discussions.

An excerpt from a deliverable I produced involving one of AC's characters, subject to revision.

Much of my work within NERDLab has been focused on building the game's narrative and world, especially as development has moved towards a complete overhaul of the game's storyline and structure. Currently, work is focused on producing the first major 'episode' of the game in the form of "Answer Campus - First Semester", which will serve as an introduction to the Answer Campus universe and its characters. In accomplishing this, I have built a strong relationship with both the team's leadership and my fellow writers.

Work is ongoing, and so I am not at liberty to share much of my work at this time, beyond some snippets. However, I am excited to see how the game will turn out and to continue working on it as part of NERDLab.
