Case Study: "Own Goal"

WORK IN PROGRESS - WILL FINISH UP

Answer Campus is an in-development visual novel that has been worked on by Professor Clay Ewing as well as members of both his CIM-444 course and, to a greater extent, the newly formed NERDLab, an in-house game development studio operating within the University of Miami where I study. I was introduced to Answer Campus through Ewing's CIM-444 course and later would involve myself directly with the development of the game through NERDLab as part of my practicum works.

Answer Campus is a social impact game aimed at simulating the average university student's experience.
